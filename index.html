How 3D & AR works

  1. 1

    Explore in 3D

    Select a product marked 3D to open the interactive viewer. Rotate, zoom and inspect the product directly in your browser.

  2. 2

    View in AR

    On supported mobile devices, select View in AR to place the product into your environment and view it at scale.

  3. 3

    Works on every screen

    View the models on desktop, tablet or mobile. Augmented reality works on Android and iOS, with no separate app to install.
