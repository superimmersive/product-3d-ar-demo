How the 3D and AR works

  1. 1

    Open in 3D

    Tap a product marked 3D. Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom. Works on any modern browser.

  2. 2

    View in AR

    On a phone, View in AR places the product full size in the room. iPhone uses Quick Look, Android uses Scene Viewer.

  3. 3

    Scan from a laptop

    On a desktop, View in AR shows a QR code. Scan it and the phone opens straight into AR.
